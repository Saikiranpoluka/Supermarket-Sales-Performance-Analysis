Supermarket Sales Performance Analysis📌 Project OverviewThis project involves a comprehensive analysis of supermarket sales data using SQL to identify the key drivers of revenue across diverse product lines.📂 Dataset AttributesThe analysis evaluates multiple attributes of the supermarket's operations, including:Geographic & Customer Data: Segment, Country, City, State, Postal-Code, and Region.Product Details: Category and Subcategory (e.g., Furniture, Technology, Chairs, Binders).Financial Metrics: Ship-Mode, Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit.🔍 Key SQL Analyses PerformedProfitability Tracking: Calculated the raw cost and margin percentages for products to determine overall profitability.Regional Performance: Identified top-performing cities in respective regions and pinpointed the top 3 profitable branches in the United States.Loss Analysis: Wrote queries to identify the top losing branches from their respective regions.Sales Volume: Determined the top sales sub-categories by quantity and unit price.Discount & Revenue Impact: Found the top 5 branches offering the highest average discounts and calculated average revenue generation for specific states, such as New York ($275.59).🛠️ Tools Used MySQL
